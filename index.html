  • Use TradingView Free to chart NSE stocks (e.g., NSE:NIFTY, NSE:RELIANCE)[cite: 41].
  • Use the Bar Replay tool for blind historical backtesting without lookahead bias[cite: 9, 13].
  • Set up 20/50 EMAs, RSI, and ATR for visual rule logging[cite: 9, 11, 12].
Virtual Execution & Ledger Paper Simulator
  • Activate TradingView Paper Trading in Month 4 to execute simulated orders with stops[cite: 22, 25].
  • Log every hypothetical setup in Google Sheets (Date, Symbol, Entry, Stop, R-multiple, Emotion)[cite: 13, 16].
